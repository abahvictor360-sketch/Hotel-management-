Why Hotel Hub

Cloud-only software stops when the internet does. Your hotel shouldn't.

The usual problem

A fibre cut or a power-hungry router, and a web-only system freezes: no check-ins, no bills, guests waiting at reception, and handwritten notes to re-enter later.

The Hotel Hub way

Settings

Security & reliability

Serious about your guests' data

Security is built into the database itself, not bolted on afterwards.

Isolated at the database

Row-level security keeps every hotel's records separate, so one hotel can never see another's data, even through a bug.

Nothing disappears

Financial records can't be edited or deleted, only reversed, and every change lands in an audit trail with the person and device.

Encrypted payment keys

Gateway secret keys are sealed on the hub so only the payment service can open them. Staff never see them.

Least privilege everywhere

Each staff role sees only what it needs. Each part of the system connects with its own restricted database account.

Registered devices only

Only enrolled terminals can sign in. Lose a tablet? Revoke it from the Devices page in seconds.

Your data stays yours

Export every record at any time as a complete archive, and back up the hub to your own drive.

FAQ

Questions hotels ask

What happens when the internet goes down?

Nothing changes for your staff. Check-ins, bills, orders and receipts all keep working on the hotel network. Changes wait in a queue and upload automatically when the connection returns. Only online bookings and the owner dashboard need the internet.

What equipment do we need?

One small computer to act as the hub, on Windows or Ubuntu, connected to your hotel network. Reception, restaurant and other staff use any browser on a PC, laptop or tablet. Thermal receipt printers on the network, USB or serial are supported.

Can we move our existing data across?

Yes. Import rooms and room types, menu items and your guest list from Excel or CSV. The hub checks the whole file first and lists any problems by row, so nothing half-imports.

How do owners check on the hotel remotely?

An administrator creates a secure, expiring access link for the owner dashboard. It shows revenue, payments, occupancy and balances from the cloud copy, read-only, without sharing any staff password.

How do online payments work?

Guests book on your own booking page and can pay through Paystack or Flutterwave into your own account. You choose whether payment is required, optional or taken at the hotel.

Is our data safe if a device is stolen?

Only registered devices can sign in. An administrator can revoke a device or deactivate a staff account at any time, and every action is recorded in the audit trail.

Can you customise it for our hotel?

Yes. Beyond branding and settings, custom features, reports and integrations are available. Tell us what you need.
